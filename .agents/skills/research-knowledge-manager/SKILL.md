# Research & Knowledge Manager

## Objective

Maintain a structured record of the project's research activities.

This skill continuously documents implementation progress, experiment outcomes,
research findings, and important technical decisions so they can later be used
for thesis writing, reports, publications, and project reviews.

This skill complements Project Memory by recording **why and what was learned**,
rather than **what the current project state is**.

---

# When to Use

Use this skill whenever:

- a major feature is completed
- an experiment finishes
- a benchmark is completed
- a research decision is made
- a new paper or technique is adopted
- a technique is rejected
- meaningful results are obtained

---

# Responsibilities

Maintain:

- Research journal
- Experiment summaries
- Implementation summaries
- Research decisions
- Lessons learned
- Literature mapping
- Future research ideas
- Thesis notes

---

# Workflow

For every research activity:

1. Review the completed work or experiment.
2. Summarize the objective.
3. Record the implementation or methodology.
4. Record the outcome.
5. Capture important observations or lessons.
6. Update the appropriate research documents.

---

# Files to Maintain

Update these files as needed:

.agents/project/research/

research_log.md

experiment_journal.md

implementation_log.md

literature_map.md

thesis_notes.md

lessons_learned.md

future_work.md

weekly_summary.md

Only update files affected by the completed work.

---

# Rules

- Record factual outcomes only.
- Clearly distinguish observations from conclusions.
- Record both successful and unsuccessful experiments.
- Never delete historical research entries.
- Link related implementations, experiments, and literature whenever possible.
- Keep entries concise and chronological.

---

# Success Criteria

The skill is successful when:

- Every significant implementation has a research entry.
- Every completed experiment has a summary.
- Important research decisions are documented.
- Lessons learned are preserved.
- Thesis notes remain up to date.
- Future research opportunities are recorded.
